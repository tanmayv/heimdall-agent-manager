HOST-4 live smoke: `ham-pty-host attach --instance <id>` single-agent raw passthrough
=====================================================================================
Setup: daemon on a unix socket, one agent "solo" running:
  /bin/sh -c 'i=0; while :; do echo LIVE_LINE_$i; i=$((i+1)); sleep 0.4; done'

Driver: pty.fork() a real terminal, exec `attach --socket <s> --instance solo`,
set winsize 30x100 (TIOCSWINSZ) so SIGWINCH -> Resize propagates, capture stdout
for ~1.8s, then send Ctrl-\ (0x1c) to detach.

Result:
  live lines captured: 5 ['LIVE_LINE_1','LIVE_LINE_2','LIVE_LINE_3','LIVE_LINE_4','LIVE_LINE_5']
  --- child survived detach? ---
  solo                     pid=46266   alive        30x100 /bin/sh

=> Live Output streams to the single-agent view; Ctrl-\ detaches WITHOUT killing
   the child; SIGWINCH resized the contained PTY to 30x100 (matches the pane).
